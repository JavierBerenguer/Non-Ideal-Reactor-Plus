8. Close recycle loops and meet design targets

Recycle compares a tear-stream estimate with its calculated return; Adjust varies one connected parameter to meet a measured target.

Before You Start

Make the open flowsheet solve without the loop where possible. Identify the calculated tear Stream, its estimate, the measured target, and the scalar parameter that may be varied.

Recycle

Connect the calculated tear Stream to Calculated and the return Stream to Estimate. Choose Direct or Wegstein, enter the relative tolerance, and choose an initial estimate. Wegstein also uses QMin, QMax, and Accelerate every N iterations.

VariablesChecks used
Method, tolerance, initial estimate, and Wegstein limits and interval when selectedTolerance must be positive, limits must be ordered, and the initial estimate must lie inside them.
  1. Insert one Recycle block at the tear Stream.
  2. Identify Calculated and Estimate Streams.
  3. Start with Direct or choose Wegstein with ordered bounds.
  4. Enter a positive tolerance and a plausible initial estimate.

A material recycle creates an algebraic loop. Insert one Recycle block at the tear Stream. Direct substitutes the calculated Stream on every iteration; Wegstein accelerates the update within its displayed bounds. Convergence requires the relative Stream error to remain below Tolerance.

An outlet-flow specification inside a recycle is applied when the loop converges for the first time; until then, the previous split is used to obtain a stable first solution. If the loop conserves total moles, specifying an absolute purge flow does not uniquely determine the purge fraction. Specify the outlet fractions in that case.

Example: example 48, PFR with recycle (R = 1, Levenspiel), demonstrates a material recycle.

Adjust

Connect the measured Stream to the Adjust input and its Parameter (SI) output to a supported scalar port. Adjust can vary reactor volume or residence time, feed temperature or flow, Jacket area or utility temperature, Heater outlet temperature or duty, Splitter fraction 1, and a Jacket specified reactor temperature or duty. Choose the target variable and value, parameter bounds, damping, tolerance, and strategy. Connect identifies an available downstream parameter port and limits the units to the matching category.

VariablesChecks used
Measured target, target value, varied parameter, bounds, initial value, damping, tolerance, and strategyTolerance is positive, bounds are ordered, the initial value lies within them, and the parameter unit matches the connected scalar port. The connected parameter is varied during Run.
  1. Connect the measured Stream.
  2. Choose the target and target value.
  3. Press Connect and select a supported parameter port.
  4. Enter bounds, initial value, damping, tolerance, and strategy.

Example: example 6, Adjusted CSTR volume, varies reactor volume to meet a target.

Non-ideal Reactor Rules

Adjust may vary V or τ for a Non-ideal Reactor only when its RTD origin is Model. A Data or Session RTD owns its mean time, so Adjust cannot change that hydraulic scale.

Recycle limitation: a material recycle loop that passes through a Non-ideal Reactor is rejected. A recycle on another branch remains available when its loop does not pass through the block.

Using Adjust and Recycle Together

When Adjust and Recycle share a model, Simultaneous updates both during the same iteration. Nested waits for every Recycle to converge before the next Adjust step. The solved example Separator recycle loop with adjusted CSTR volume demonstrates Nested operation.

Frequent Errors

If a loop does not converge, inspect Recycle and Messages in Show results. Check topology and specifications before increasing Maximum iterations; then improve the initial estimate or try Wegstein with conservative bounds.

MessageCorrection
Maximum iterations reached or did not convergeRead the Recycle and Adjust rows plus Messages, check specifications and tear placement, improve the initial estimate, then consider Wegstein or Nested and a larger iteration limit.
