Appendix

Glossary and Symbols Used Across the App

This appendix collects the notation and recurring model names that appear across tabs, tables, and plots. It is meant as a fast orientation page when the same symbol keeps reappearing in different contexts.

RTD and Moment Symbols

Symbol or label Meaning in the app
E(t)Residence time distribution in dimensional time.
F(t)Cumulative residence time distribution.
E(θ)Residence time distribution in normalized time.
τMean residence time.
σ2Variance in dimensional time.
σθ2Normalized variance.

Convolution and Deconvolution Symbols

Symbol or labelMeaning in the app
Cin(t)Tracer concentration entering the vessel.
Cout(t)Measured or calculated tracer concentration leaving the vessel.
ΔtcommonSampling interval of the common time grid used for the signals.
τmin, τmaxLower and upper limits of the estimated RTD support.
λTikhonov regularization strength used during deconvolution.
Relative residualReconvolution error relative to the measured outlet magnitude.
AEArea of the estimated E(t) over the selected support; a physical RTD should have unit area.

Equivalent-Model Parameters

Symbol or label Interpretation
NEquivalent number of equal CSTRs in the Tanks-in-Series route.
PePeclet number used in the Dispersion Model UI.
BoInternal reciprocal dispersion number, typically 1/Pe.
Closed-closedDispersion-model boundary condition with the general reactive route currently available.
Open-openDispersion-model boundary condition restricted to the exact supported kinetics check.

Reactive Comparison Labels

Label How to read it
SegregationReactive limit based on the RTD with no ideal micromixing between fluid elements of different ages.
Maximum MixednessThe opposite micromixing limit relative to Segregation for the same RTD.
Ideal CSTRPerfectly mixed ideal reactor reference.
Ideal PFRPlug-flow ideal reactor reference.
Segregated CSTR RTDSegregation applied to the theoretical RTD of an ideal CSTR for the map in Prediction Models.

Species and Metric Labels

Label Meaning
C_inInlet concentration of a species.
C_outOutlet concentration of a species.
XConversion, meaningful for functional reactants.
SelectivityProduct-oriented metric defined relative to the chosen reactant basis in the current route.
YieldCombined measure of reactant consumption and desired-product formation.

When explicit component names are unavailable, the app falls back to names such as CA, CB, CC, and so on.

Name Role
