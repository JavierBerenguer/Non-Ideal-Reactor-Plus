Tab 3 - Tanks-in-Series

This tab replaces the full RTD by a cascade of equal CSTRs in series. It is useful when you want a compact equivalent model that still reflects RTD spread through the parameter N.

What You Need Before Using It

  • An RTD, either imported from Tab 1 or defined directly for this route.
  • A ReactionSys and a Stream if you want reactive results.
Best use case: choose this tab when you want a more interpretable surrogate than a full experimental RTD, especially if you need to communicate the hydrodynamics as "equivalent mixing severity" rather than as a full curve.

Main Ideas

  • Low N means broader residence-time spread and stronger backmixing.
  • High N approaches plug-flow behavior.
  • The tab can infer N from the RTD variance or use a manual value.
Engineering meaning: this tab is often easier to communicate than a full experimental RTD because a single cascade size gives a familiar surrogate for the degree of mixing, even though it is still an approximation.

Main Controls and What They Mean

Control or mode How to read it
Manual / From Calculated DataDecides whether the tab is fed directly from previously computed RTD data or configured locally.
N modeLets you infer N from RTD spread or impose a manual cascade size.
ReactionSys and Stream loadingProvide the chemistry and inlet state needed for reactive tables and sweep plots.
Display selectorsControl which species or metric is emphasized in the sweep views without changing the underlying solution set.

In practice, the most important modeling decision here is whether the inferred N is physically meaningful enough for your case or whether the full RTD route from Prediction Models is still the more honest representation.

For a broader comparison against Dispersion Model and the direct RTD route, see the Model Selection guide.

What the Tab Shows

  • An E(t) view for the equivalent cascade.
  • Reactive comparisons against ideal references.
  • A summary table for inlet and outlet concentrations and conversions.
Panel Use it for
Outlet Concentration vs NChecking how sensitive each selected species is to the degree of backmixing represented by N.
Conversion vs NSeeing where the current fitted or imported N sits relative to the CSTR and PFR limits.
E(t) - TIS ModelVisualizing the equivalent RTD shape associated with the current N.

For help deciding whether a visible difference is large enough to matter, use the Results Interpretation appendix.

Typical Workflow

  1. Bring in the RTD and chemistry.
  2. Choose whether N is inferred from the RTD or set manually.
  3. Press Compute.
  4. Use the sweep curves to judge whether the equivalent model behaves closer to the CSTR or PFR limit.

When the tab is especially useful

  • When a one-parameter hydrodynamic surrogate is enough.
  • When you want to explain non-ideality through progressive backmixing.
  • When the audience already understands cascades of stirred tanks.

When Tab 2 may still be better

  • When the exact RTD shape itself matters.
  • When you do not want to compress the RTD into a single surrogate parameter.
  • When the fit quality of an equivalent cascade is not convincing.

Typical Mistakes

  • Reading the equivalent N as a literal number of physical tanks instead of an RTD-based surrogate.
  • Forgetting that a restored session may need a fresh Compute to repopulate the graphs.
  • Comparing the tab directly with Tab 2 without remembering that TIS is an equivalent-model route, not the original full RTD route.
